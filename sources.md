# Sources

The frozen bibliography. Each entry in the form it takes in the paper's `## References`, with a one-line provenance note. All locators web-verified against the publisher record on 2026-07-15 unless marked. Every in-text `et al.` maps to a co-authored entry here.

## Bibliography

Berger, A., and Hill, T. P. (2011). A basic theory of Benford's Law. *Probability Surveys*, 8, 1--126. — verified (Project Euclid, DOI 10.1214/11-PS175); supports the Benford significand law and its invariance conditions (case study II).

Bongard, J., and Levin, M. (2023). There's plenty of room right here: biological systems as evolved, overloaded, multi-scale machines. *Biomimetics*, 8(1), 110. — verified (MDPI, DOI 10.3390/biomimetics8010110); supports polycomputing / polyfunctionality.

Candès, E. J., Romberg, J., and Tao, T. (2006). Robust uncertainty principles: exact signal reconstruction from highly incomplete frequency information. *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory*, 52(2), 489--509. — verified; supports compression subsidy (compressed sensing).

Clark, A., and Chalmers, D. (1998). The extended mind. *Analysis*, 58(1), 7--19. — verified; supports externalization (offloading into environment).

Cohen, T., and Welling, M. (2016). Group equivariant convolutional networks. In *Proceedings of the 33rd International Conference on Machine Learning*, PMLR 48, 2990--2999. — verified (PMLR); supports equivalence (symmetry lowers sample cost).

Donoho, D. L. (2006). Compressed sensing. *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory*, 52(4), 1289--1306. — verified; supports compression subsidy alongside Candès et al.

Draghi, J. A., Parsons, T. L., Wagner, G. P., and Plotkin, J. B. (2010). Mutational robustness can facilitate adaptation. *Nature*, 463(7279), 353--355. — verified (DOI 10.1038/nature08694); supports the robustness-flexibility regime dependence (H6).

Durant, F., Morokuma, J., Fields, C., Williams, K., Adams, D. S., and Levin, M. (2017). Long-term, stochastic editing of regenerative anatomy via targeting endogenous bioelectric gradients. *Biophysical Journal*, 112(10), 2231--2243. — verified (Cell/PMC5443973); flagship biological evidence, case study IV.

Feigenbaum, M. J. (1978). Quantitative universality for a class of nonlinear transformations. *Journal of Statistical Physics*, 19(1), 25--52. — verified; supports coarse-graining / universality subsidy.

Finn, C., Abbeel, P., and Levine, S. (2017). Model-agnostic meta-learning for fast adaptation of deep networks. In *Proceedings of the 34th International Conference on Machine Learning*, PMLR 70, 1126--1135. — verified (PMLR). Note: Levine, not Levin. Supports amortization (upstream cost hidden in a fast-adapting initialization).

Gould, S. J., and Vrba, E. S. (1982). Exaptation, a missing term in the science of form. *Paleobiology*, 8(1), 4--15. — verified; supports exaptation as a discount mechanism.

Gumuskaya, G., Srivastava, P., Cooper, B. G., Lesser, H., Semegran, B., Garnier, S., and Levin, M. (2024). Motile living biobots self-construct from adult human somatic progenitor seed cells. *Advanced Science*, 11(4), 2303575. — verified (Wiley, DOI 10.1002/advs.202303575; journal issue 2024); Anthrobots, case study IV.

Hamming, R. W. (1950). Error detecting and error correcting codes. *Bell System Technical Journal*, 29(2), 147--160. — verified; supports the protection transformation (error-correcting codes).

Kashtan, N., and Alon, U. (2005). Spontaneous evolution of modularity and network motifs. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, 102(39), 13773--13778. — verified (PNAS, DOI 10.1073/pnas.0503610102); modularly varying goals, research program Experiment 5.

Kriegman, S., Blackiston, D., Levin, M., and Bongard, J. (2021). Kinematic self-replication in reconfigurable organisms. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, 118(49), e2112672118. — verified (PNAS, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2112672118); Xenobot kinematic replication, case study IV.

Levin, M. (2022). Technological approach to mind everywhere: an experimentally-grounded framework for understanding diverse bodies and minds. *Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience*, 16, 768201. — verified; TAME / basal-cognition continuum, competent components.

Levin, M. (2026). A short argument on Platonic Space. Blog post, thoughtforms.life. — non-peer-reviewed; the interpretive target the paper reframes. Cited as a position, not as evidence.

Li, M., and Vitányi, P. (2008). *An Introduction to Kolmogorov Complexity and Its Applications* (3rd ed.). Springer. — canonical reference; supports the algorithmic-information bound (no targeted information from a closed program beyond fixed overhead).

Lu, L., Joannopoulos, J. D., and Soljačić, M. (2014). Topological photonics. *Nature Photonics*, 8(11), 821--829. — verified; supports topological protection as the clean physical calibration case.

McGeer, T. (1990). Passive dynamic walking. *International Journal of Robotics Research*, 9(2), 62--82. — verified; passive walking, case study III.

Nakajima, K., Hauser, H., Li, T., and Pfeifer, R. (2015). Information processing via physical soft body. *Scientific Reports*, 5, 10487. — verified (Nature, DOI 10.1038/srep10487); physical reservoir computing, case study III.

Odling-Smee, F. J., Laland, K. N., and Feldman, M. W. (2003). *Niche Construction: The Neglected Process in Evolution*. Princeton University Press. — canonical; supports externalization with lock-in.

Rissanen, J. (1978). Modeling by shortest data description. *Automatica*, 14(5), 465--471. — verified; MDL, supports compression and the matched-prior reading of NFL.

Rothemund, P. W. K. (2006). Folding DNA to create nanoscale shapes and patterns. *Nature*, 440(7082), 297--302. — verified; DNA origami, self-assembly / delegation.

Sheffer, H. M. (1913). A set of five independent postulates for Boolean algebras, with application to logical constants. *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society*, 14(4), 481--488. — verified; the single Boolean basis, case study I.

Theraulaz, G., and Bonabeau, E. (1999). A brief history of stigmergy. *Artificial Life*, 5(2), 97--116. — verified; stigmergy, delegation via environmental traces.

Turing, A. M. (1952). The chemical basis of morphogenesis. *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society of London B*, 237(641), 37--72. — verified; reaction-diffusion, delegation and case study IV.

Wolpert, D. H., and Macready, W. G. (1997). No free lunch theorems for optimization. *IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation*, 1(1), 67--82. — verified; the licensing argument (uniform averaging vs structured worlds).

Zaheer, M., Kottur, S., Ravanbakhsh, S., Póczos, B., Salakhutdinov, R., and Smola, A. J. (2017). Deep Sets. *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems*, 30, 3391--3401. — verified (NeurIPS); permutation-invariant function classes, equivalence.

Zhang, T., Goldstein, A., and Levin, M. (2024). Classical sorting algorithms as a model of morphogenesis: self-sorting arrays reveal unexpected competencies in a minimal model of basal intelligence. *arXiv preprint* arXiv:2401.05375. — verified; minimal model with full code and intervention history, case study IV.
